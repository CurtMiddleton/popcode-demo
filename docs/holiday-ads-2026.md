## 1. Instagram / Facebook video ad (do this first)

### What you need
- **A real ornament in hand.** If you don't have one, order one today (Scout's or a family
  photo). Until it arrives, film with the photo book or a print of
  `popcode.app/assets/scout-ornament.pdf` cut out and hung on a branch, and swap in the real
  ornament when it comes.
- Two phones: one to film, one to scan with (the scanning phone's screen must be bright).
- A tree or a few pine branches, warm lights, daylight from a window.

### Ad A — "It plays" (the main one), 9–12 seconds, vertical 9:16
| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Close-up: the ornament turning slowly on the tree. | **This ornament plays a video.** |
| 2–5 s | Over-the-shoulder: a hand raises a phone to the ornament; the camera view finds it. | *(none — let it happen)* |
| 5–9 s | The video plays on the phone screen, held next to the ornament. Sound ON (a laugh, a bark, "Merry Christmas!"). | **Point a phone at it. No app.** |
| 9–12 s | Pull back: the tree, the phone still playing. | **Your photo. Your video.** popcode.app |

Tips: the first 2 seconds decide everything, so start on the ornament already in motion, not
on a logo. Film the phone screen in a dim room so it doesn't glare. Add captions; most people
watch muted. Keep the clip that plays short and emotional (a child or a dog beats a landscape).

### Ad B — "Grandma hears them", 12–15 s
Film a grandparent (yours, a friend's — with permission) opening the ornament or calendar,
pointing the phone, and reacting when the grandkids' video plays. The reaction *is* the ad.
Text: **She pointed her phone at the photo…** → (reaction) → **…and heard them say Merry Christmas.**
→ **Gifts that play. popcode.app**

### Ad C — "The book", 10 s (re-use what you have)
`public/video/Album.mp4` already shows a phone over the open photo book. Add text:
**A photo book where the photos play videos.** → **No app. No QR codes.** → **popcode.app**

## Tracking — how to tell if it's working
- Every ad link carries `utm_source=google|meta` and `utm_medium=cpc|paid_social`; Analytics
  counts these under **Search & AI** (Google) and **Social** (Meta) even when the click id is lost.
- Sign-ups record their source; check Analytics → Accounts for "Campaign: google / meta".
- Rough bar after 2 weeks (~$420 spent): if neither network has produced a single order or
  a handful of sign-ups, stop that one and move its budget to the other.

## Not set up (decide later)
- **Meta Pixel / Google tag:** would let each network optimise for purchases, but both set
  cookies and send visitor data to Meta/Google, which cuts against the cookie-free analytics
  and the privacy promises on /nonprofits. Worth it only if the first two weeks show the
  ads can sell; add on the shop/checkout pages only if so.
