Questions

Is there an app to download?

No. The person you give it to opens a short web address in their phone's browser, points the camera at the picture, and it plays. No install, no account, nothing to sign up for.

Is there a QR code on the picture?

No — and that's deliberate. The picture itself is what the camera recognises, so nothing gets printed over your photo or stuck next to your painting. There's a small Popcode symbol to let people know it's scannable, and the web address, but the image stays exactly as you made it.

How does someone know where to go?

The short address goes on the piece — on the back of a print, inside a book's cover, on a card beside a painting. It looks like popcode.app/maya. That's the one thing they have to see, so we'd rather say it plainly than pretend it isn't there.

Can I use my voice instead of a video?

Yes, and about half of what people make is audio. You can record a narration straight into the browser. It's the right answer for old photographs where no footage exists — a wedding album from 1985, a portrait of someone who's gone, a painting you want to explain in your own words.

Does it work on paintings and prints, not just photos?

Yes. Anything printed and reasonably detailed works — photographs, paintings, illustrations, book pages, posters, album sleeves. Flat, evenly lit and not behind heavy glare is best. Very plain or repetitive surfaces are the hard case.

How long does it keep working?

Every Popcode includes five years of hosting from the day you make it, and we'll email you before that's up so you can renew. Printed products include the same five years in the purchase price.

What does it cost?

Printed products are priced per item in the shop — one payment, hosting included. Making your own is free while we're in beta. When that changes we'll say so well in advance, and nothing you've already made will stop working without warning.
