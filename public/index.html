How it works

Everything, in more detail.

What is Popcode?

Popcode brings printed photos to life. Using augmented reality, it lets you attach a video, a voice recording or a montage to any photo — so when someone points their phone camera at that photo, it plays right on screen.

Think of it like a QR code, but instead of a boring link, the photo itself becomes the trigger. Wedding albums that play a highlight reel. Family photos narrated in a grandparent's own voice. Art prints that come alive.

And you can order the printed pieces here too — photo books, wall calendars, framed prints, canvas and more — with the Popcode symbol added for you, so everything arrives ready to scan.

No app download required — it works right in the browser.

How do I create a Popcode?
  1. Sign up for an account Create a free account at popcode.app using your email address.
  2. Start a new Popcode Give it a name — for example, "Addie's First Year" or "Summer Trip 2025." Each photo you add inside it becomes its own scannable Popcode.
  3. Add your photos, and what each one plays For each photo, upload the image that will be scanned, then choose what plays when someone scans it — a video, a voice recording, or a montage built from your photos. Add as many as you like.
  4. Share your link Once your Popcode is ready, you'll get a short link like popcode.app/abc123. Share it with anyone you want to be able to view your Popcode. You can also just give them the code (e.g. "abc123") and tell them to go to popcode.app and enter it.
  5. Print them, or have us print them Download your photos with the Popcode symbol already on them and print them yourself — or order prints, photo books, calendars and wall art from the Shop, and we'll add the symbol for you. Either way, anyone with your link can scan them.

What can play when someone scans?

Every photo is set up on its own, so one Popcode can mix all three.

  • A video Upload a video from your phone or computer. This is the classic Popcode — the photo stays still, then starts moving.
  • A voice recording Record audio right in the browser — no file needed. Lovely for a grandparent narrating an old photo, a note on a fridge magnet, or a message tucked into a card. The photo stays on screen while the recording plays.
  • A montage Pick a set of photos and we build a memory video from them — with gentle motion, your choice of shape, and music if you want it. Good when you have plenty of photos but no video.

How do people know to scan my photo?

That's what the Popcode symbol is for — a small visual cue that tells people the photo is alive and ready to scan.

It gets onto your photos in one of two ways:

  • Order from the Shop and we add it Anything you order — prints, books, calendars, wall art — is printed with the symbol already in place. Nothing for you to do.
  • Printing them yourself? Download them with the symbol on Open a Popcode from My Popcodes and tap Download. You get your photos with the symbol baked in, ready to send to a printer, and you can pick how large it sits — small, medium or large.
How do I scan a Popcoded image?
  1. Open the link Open the Popcode link you were given — either by tapping it directly or by going to popcode.app and entering the code.
  2. Tap "Scan image" Allow camera access when prompted, then tap the button to start scanning.
  3. Point your camera at a photo Hold your phone steady and point it at one of the photos carrying the Popcode symbol. Make sure the whole photo is visible in the frame.
  4. Watch it come to life The video starts playing automatically. If the photo has a voice recording instead, you'll see the photo on screen with audio controls. Tap the X to close and scan another photo.
Having trouble scanning?

Here are a few tips if the video isn't triggering:

  • Make sure the whole photo is visible Hold your phone so the entire photo fits within the camera frame, with good lighting.
  • Twist or shake your phone slightly Sometimes a small movement — a gentle twist or shake — is enough to get the scanner to register the photo.
  • Refresh the page Pull down to refresh or tap the refresh button in your browser, then tap Scan again.
  • Still having trouble? Email us at info@popcodeapp.com and we'll help you out.
