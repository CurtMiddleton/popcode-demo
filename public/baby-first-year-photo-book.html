Questions

Does the person looking need an app?

No. They open the short popcode.app address printed on the piece in their phone’s web browser, allow the camera, and point it at the photo. It works on iPhone and Android, with nothing to download and no account to make.

Can a photo play a voice recording instead of a video?

Yes. Any photo can play a video clip or an audio recording, like the baby babbling or a song you sing them.

Is the board book sturdy enough for a baby?

It’s a real 6×6″ board book: a front cover and 18 square pages of laminated chipboard, made for little hands.

How long do the videos stay online?

Every Popcode comes with five years of hosting from the day you make it. Before that runs out, we email you so you can renew it or download your photos and videos. Nothing is switched off without a word from us first.

Can I change a video after it’s printed?

Yes. The print stays the same, but you can swap what any photo plays from your Popcode account at any time.

What does it cost?

You pay for the printed product; the videos linked to anything you buy from the Popcode Shop are free and unlimited. To add videos to photos you already have, your first five are free; see Pricing.

Do you ship in the US?

Yes. Popcode is a US company and ships to addresses across the United States.
