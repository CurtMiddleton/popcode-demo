How it works

You do the making. They just point their phone.

Pick the photos

Lay out a calendar or photo book in your browser, or choose one photo for an ornament or mug.

Add the videos

Link a clip from your phone to each photo, or have the kids record a voice message.

We print and ship it

To you or straight to the grandparents, with its own short address, like popcode.app/love-from-us, printed on it.

They point a phone

They open that address in their phone’s browser and point it at a photo. The video plays. Nothing to install.

Why grandparents love it

  • It’s a real thing to hold. Not another link in the family group chat that scrolls away.
  • The voices are in it. Photos show how the kids look; the videos keep how they sounded at this age.
  • Nothing new to learn. No app, no account, no QR codes. The phone’s browser and camera do it.
  • It keeps going. You can change what a photo plays later, so the gift can keep getting new videos.

Questions

Do my parents need an app or a new phone?

No app. Any recent iPhone or Android phone with a camera and a web browser works. They open the short popcode.app address printed on the gift, allow the camera, and point it at a photo.

Is it hard for grandparents to use?

There is one step to remember: open the address printed on the gift and point the phone at a photo. If you can, show them once in person, or on a video call when the gift arrives. After that the address can live in their phone as a bookmark.

Can a photo play a voice message instead of a video?

Yes. Any photo can play a video clip or an audio recording, like the kids saying good night or singing a song.

Can I add new videos after it's printed?

Yes. The printed gift stays the same, but you can change what any photo plays from your Popcode account at any time. A calendar photo can play something new each month.

What does it cost?

You pay for the printed gift. The videos linked to anything you buy from the Popcode Shop are free and unlimited. Each product page shows its starting price, and shipping is added at checkout.

Can you ship it straight to the grandparents?

Yes. Enter their address at checkout. Popcode ships to addresses across the United States.

When should I order for Christmas?

Everything is printed to order. A single ornament or mug is quick to make; a photo book or calendar takes longer to put together and to print, so start those first and order early in December.

How long do the videos stay online?

Every Popcode comes with five years of hosting from the day you make it. Before that runs out, Popcode emails you so you can renew it or download your photos and videos.
