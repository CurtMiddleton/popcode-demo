Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Popcode, Inc. ("Popcode," "we," "us," or "our") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We aim to keep this short and human.

What we collect

  • Account info: name, email, password (hashed) when you sign up.
  • Project content: photos, videos, audio recordings, project names, and cover images you upload or record.
  • Order info: if you order a print, book, calendar or other physical product — your shipping name and address, your email, and the images being printed. Payment card details go straight to our payment processor; they never reach Popcode's servers.
  • Usage data: when a project is scanned, what device/browser was used, and which target was matched. We use this to help creators understand how their projects are being viewed.
  • Beta feedback: any messages you send through the Beta Feedback widget, plus your email if you provide it.

How we use it

  • To run the Service — host your projects and deliver videos to viewers who scan them.
  • To produce and ship any physical products you order.
  • To send transactional email (account confirmation, password reset, beta feedback acknowledgments).
  • To improve Popcode based on aggregate usage patterns and feedback.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of companies to operate Popcode. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job, and each processes data on our behalf under its own privacy commitments.

  • Supabase (runs on AWS) — stores accounts, project records, and uploaded photos, videos and audio.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the site.
  • Stripe — processes payments. Stripe receives your payment details and billing information directly; we receive only a confirmation and the last few digits of the card.
  • Prodigi and Printify — print and ship physical products. They receive your shipping name and address and the images to be printed.
  • Shotstack — renders montage videos. If you build a montage, the photos you selected are sent there to be assembled into a video.
  • Replicate — powers image recognition on projects that use it. Where enabled, project photos and the camera frame a viewer captures are sent there to be matched. Camera frames are used for the match and are not stored by us.
  • Resend — delivers our transactional email.
  • Sentry — collects error reports so we can fix crashes. These can include your browser type and the page you were on.

Where it's stored

Popcode is a US company and our providers store data primarily in the United States. If you order from outside the US, your name and address are sent to a print partner that may fulfil your order in another country — this is how we ship internationally. By ordering, you're asking us to make that transfer so we can deliver to you.

How long we keep it

Project content stays until you delete the project or your account. Order records are kept for accounting and tax purposes even after an account is closed — we're required to retain them. Analytics are kept in a form no longer linked to your account once you delete it.

Sharing

Anyone with your project's short URL can view that project — that's the point. Treat the link as you would the content itself. We don't share your account info or analytics with anyone outside Popcode except as described above or as required by law.

Cookies and local storage

Popcode uses cookies and browser local storage only to keep you signed in and to remember small UI preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete a project: any time from My Popcodes.
  • Delete your account: from My Account, or by emailing info@popcodeapp.com. This removes your account, your projects, and the photos, videos and audio behind them from storage. Once deleted, links to those projects stop working and the content cannot be recovered.
  • Access, correct, or export your data: email info@popcodeapp.com and we'll help.
  • Email preferences: transactional email (sign-in, reset, etc.) is required to operate the account. We don't currently send marketing email.

If you're in the UK, EU, or a US state with its own privacy law, you have rights over your personal data — including access, correction, deletion, and objecting to certain uses. Use the same address above and we'll action it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Popcode is not directed to children under 13 and children may not create accounts. If you believe a child has created an account, email info@popcodeapp.com and we will remove it.

Popcode is often used for family projects, so accounts are frequently used to upload photos and videos of children. If you upload content featuring someone else — a child or anyone else — you're responsible for having the right to do so, and you control who you share the link with.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll let you know through the Service or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent change.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email info@popcodeapp.com.
