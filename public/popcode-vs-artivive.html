Questions

Is Popcode an alternative to Artivive?

For printed photo products, yes. Artivive is an augmented-reality platform for adding animation and video to artwork and images you already have. Popcode also makes the printed piece: you design a photo book, calendar or print, link a video to each photo, and Popcode prints and ships it.

Do viewers need an app?

With Popcode, no: viewers open a short popcode.app address in the phone's web browser. Artivive offers both its free app and, on its paid Pro Plus plan and above, browser-based viewing (WebAR) opened by scanning a QR code.

Can I use Artivive with a photo book from another company?

Yes, that is a common do-it-yourself route: order the book from a photo-book company, then set up each photo in Artivive. It works, but it means two services, and the book doesn't carry any instructions unless you add them.

Which is better for artists and galleries?

Artivive is built for artists and creative AR work, with tools for animation and layered effects. Popcode plays a video or voice recording straight from the photo or painting. For galleries and museums that want a simple audio tour, see popcode.app/exhibitions.
