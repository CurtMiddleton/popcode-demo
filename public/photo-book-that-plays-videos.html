Why not a regular photo book and an app?

You can put video in a printed book a few ways. A common one is to order a book from a regular photo-book company and then link the videos yourself, either with an augmented-reality app or by printing a QR code beside each photo. That works, but it means two or three services, and the people looking at the book need to know which one to use.

Popcode is one service for the whole thing:

  • All in one place. You design the book, link the videos and order the print on popcode.app.
  • No app for your viewers. It runs in the phone’s web browser, on iPhone or Android.
  • The photo is the trigger. There are no QR codes printed next to your pictures.
  • Ships to your door in the US. Ready to give, with the address already printed in it.

Questions

Do the people looking at the book need an app?

No. They open the short popcode.app address printed in the book in their phone’s web browser, allow the camera, and point it at a photo. There is nothing to download and no account to make.

Are there QR codes on the photos?

No. The photo itself is what the phone recognises. A small Popcode symbol in the corner shows which photos play, and the web address is printed once in the book.

Can a photo play a voice recording instead of a video?

Yes. Any photo can play a video clip or an audio recording, such as a grandparent telling the story behind the picture.

How long do the videos stay online?

Every Popcode comes with five years of hosting from the day you make it. Before that runs out, we email you so you can renew it or download your photos and videos. Nothing is switched off without a word from us first.

What does it cost?

You pay for the printed product. The videos linked to anything you buy from the Popcode Shop are free and unlimited. Each product page shows its starting price, and shipping is added at checkout. If you’d rather add videos to photos you already have, your first five are free; see Pricing.

Do you ship in the US?

Yes. Popcode is a US company and ships to addresses across the United States.

Can I change a video after the book is printed?

Yes. The printed page stays the same, but you can swap what a photo plays from your Popcode account at any time.

Does it work with old photos?

Yes, old or new. Photos with plenty of detail are the easiest for a phone to recognise; very plain or very dark pictures are harder.
