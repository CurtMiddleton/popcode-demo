Paid by bank transfer? It takes a few business days to clear. There's no need to wait: send your materials now and we'll get started.

What we need from you

  1. Your videos

    One short video for each story: your stories. Filmed on a phone is perfect. Videos are usually too big to attach, so share a folder link (Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer). Haven't filmed yet? Tell us, and we'll send a simple script and filming tips.

  2. Your images

    Your logo, and the photos you want on the printed piece. These are the photos donors will scan, so send full-size originals, not screenshots. Brand colors and fonts too, if you have them.

  3. Your donation page link

    The page the Donate button should open. If you'd like other buttons after each story, such as volunteer or sign up, send those links too.

Email your materials

Or write to hello@popcodeapp.com. Tell us your mail or event date too, so we can plan the schedule around it.

What happens next

Delivery is about four weeks after we receive your videos, images and links, including one round of revisions. You handle printing and postage.

The second half of your Gala & Donor Kit ($2,250) is invoiced on delivery. Stripe will email the invoice, and you can pay it by card or US bank transfer.
